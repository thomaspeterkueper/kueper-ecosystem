## Gewünschte Änderung

Register Planetary Evolution / Geosphere–Biosphere Coupling as a cross-project program.

Ownership:
- OTA: evidence, proxies, provenance and research uncertainty.
- KG: canonical processes, proxies, states and causal-hypothesis relations.
- Engineering: multi-timescale coupled-system contracts.
- NOXIA Universe: planetary-history and xenobiological canon.
- NOXIA Game: procedural history, scientific reconstruction gameplay and Deep Time learning.
- NOXIA Realtime: later visualization after contracts stabilize.

Cross-link with planetary remote sensing, system generation, geology/resources, biospheres, settlement science and future terraforming. Preserve federated ownership and the distinction between hidden history and reconstructed knowledge.
