# EXT-ECO-KUE-20261006-001 — Public Knowledge Anchor + Backfill

## Anlass

kueper.com is registered and enabled, but its last knowledge-foundation activity ended on
2026-09-28 at main commit `6206045841b12044f82927383621c6c09d2cd37c`.
Since then the ecosystem has accumulated substantial real-world scientific and engineering
knowledge in OTA, KG, SSF, Engineering and Products without a corresponding public,
stable reference layer.

## Zielrolle

kueper.com is the public, human-readable and citable knowledge-reference layer.

- OTA: evidence, sources and archival provenance
- KG: structured concepts, models and relations
- SSF: learning, demonstrations and research
- Engineering: technical application and validation
- Products: productization and prior-art/product decisions
- kueper.com: stable public synthesis/reference

This task does **not** make kueper.com the source of truth for evidence, KG concepts,
engineering designs or product decisions.

## Promotion gate

Introduce a `Public Knowledge Candidate` stage. A candidate may propose a new or updated
KUE-SCI foundation, but publication requires explicit evidence/provenance checks.

Minimum metadata:
- upstream source identifiers/URLs or repository paths
- evidence status / epistemic status
- last-reviewed date
- distinction between observation, model, interpretation and extrapolation
- public-safe summary
- related KG/OTA/SSF/ENG/PRODUCTS references where available

Research candidates, hypotheses and speculative model content must remain labelled as such
and MUST NOT be promoted automatically to established public knowledge.

## Backfill boundary

Inventory material created or materially changed after:
`kueper.com@6206045841b12044f82927383621c6c09d2cd37c` (2026-09-28).

## Required outputs

1. A machine-readable/publication-candidate inventory with source project, source ref,
   evidence status, proposed KUE-SCI action (new/update/none), and reason.
2. A human-readable backfill report grouped by domain.
3. Explicit `do-not-publish-yet` entries for unresolved Research Candidates.
4. Routing documentation so future eligible OTA/KG/SSF/ENG/PRODUCTS results can create
   Public Knowledge Candidates without direct publication.
5. Add kueper.com activity and public-knowledge backlog to the regular ecosystem/morning
   monitoring surface.

## Non-goals / safety boundary

- no automatic canonization
- no automatic publication
- no conversion of a Research Candidate into established fact
- no architecture decision hidden in content promotion
- no copying private manuscript material
- no publication merely because NOXIA uses a model
