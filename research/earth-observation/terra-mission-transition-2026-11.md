# NASA Terra: planned end of science acquisition (2026-12-01)

- **Recorded:** 2026-11-18 (Europe/Berlin)
- **Source event:** NASA announcement dated 2026-11-17
- **Primary source:** https://www.nasa.gov/news/recently-published/ (NASA news listing, “NASA’s Terra Mission to End”)

## Observation
NASA announced that its Terra Earth-observation spacecraft, operating for approximately 27 years after a six-year planned mission, is scheduled to stop collecting science data on **2026-12-01**. The platform has supported studies of atmosphere, land, ocean and biosphere.

## Interpretation and research relevance
For the KUEPER knowledge graph, Earth observation and climate provenance, store **announcement date**, **planned cessation date**, **actual last observation date (unknown)**, **dataset coverage**, **instrument**, **processing version** and **successor data source** as separate fields. A mission ending does **not** mean its historical archive disappears; the continuity and comparability of replacement measurements require independent validation.
