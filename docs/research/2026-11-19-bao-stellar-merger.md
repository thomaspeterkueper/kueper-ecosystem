# Stellar merger AT 2025abao (Bao) — evidence note

Date recorded: 2026-11-19. Source publication: 2026-11-18.

## Observed evidence (high confidence in reported measurements)
Multi-instrument monitoring of AT 2025abao in the Andromeda galaxy: NEOWISE archival observations, WINTER, SPHEREx, ZTF, Swift, Gemini North, and JWST (2025-12-24). A rapid optical eruption occurred in October 2025. JWST mid-infrared data support a stellar merger/common-envelope interpretation. The team published the analysis in Nature on 2026-11-18.

## Inference/model (moderate confidence; model dependent)
A ~7-solar-mass giant with ~350 solar radii engulfed a companion estimated near 0.7 solar masses. Reconstruction combines observations with simulations; the mass and history are not direct measurements. The future post-merger state is unknown.

## Forecast (not observation)
The upcoming Roman time-domain surveys may identify more similar transients; no discovery rate is yet established by this event.
